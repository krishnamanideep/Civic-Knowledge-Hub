## About the Journal

### Journal of Politics, Society and Governance

The *Journal of Politics, Society and Governance (JPSG)* is a peer-reviewed academic journal published by the Democratic Organisation for Civic Knowledge Foundation.

The journal provides a platform for original research, theoretical contributions, empirical studies, policy analysis and scholarly discussions concerning contemporary political, social and governance issues.

The journal welcomes interdisciplinary research that contributes to the understanding of political institutions, democratic processes, public policy, governance, constitutional studies, social justice, human rights and contemporary social transformations.

### Publication Frequency

The journal intends to publish four issues annually.

### Language

The primary language of publication is English.

### Peer Review

All research articles submitted to the journal undergo editorial screening and peer review in accordance with the journal's peer-review policy.

### Open Access

The journal aims to make published research accessible to researchers, students, academics and the wider public through an online publication model.

 PEER REVIEW POLICY

All manuscripts submitted to the journal undergo an initial
editorial screening.

Manuscripts considered suitable for the journal are sent to
independent reviewers.

The journal follows a double-blind peer-review process,
where possible, in which:

• The identity of the author is concealed from reviewers.
• The identity of reviewers is concealed from authors.

Reviewers evaluate manuscripts on the basis of:

• Originality
• Methodology
• Theoretical contribution
• Quality of analysis
• Relevance
• Academic writing
• References
• Overall contribution to the field

The final publication decision rests with the Editor-in-Chief
and editorial team.
